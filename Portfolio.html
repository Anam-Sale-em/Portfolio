AI Upwork proposal & video pipeline

Turns a curated Upwork job into a ready-to-send proposal and a multi-scene AI video with no manual writing. Helpables Leads Platform (GrowthLine)

Lead ingestion + multi-channel AI outreach

A two-stage system. A scheduled pipeline ingests weekly lead-research spreadsheets from Google Drive into Firestore — deduping processed files and batching writes across collections. A companion app then drives outreach: on approval an agent drafts a personalized message and follow-up under strict anti-hallucination guardrails, reviewers request in-place revisions, and the approved message reaches each lead on the right channel — SMS, AI voice call, or email. Built to serve any agency, not one hardcoded sender.
